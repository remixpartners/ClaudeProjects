## Why Lyra Is Strategically Significant

1. **Demand-side proof of SMB moat** - $1B ARR PE platform with 16K customers cannot solve AI deployment alone. Validates structural moat: AI strategy layer sits between customer relationships and implementation. FDEs at $500K don't pencil for SMBs.

2. **Recurring revenue at scale** - Pivoted from $35K one-time to opt-in "scouts" monthly subscription (monthly fee, annual renewal). Brad Morehead (Shore) named this "interesting to investable" threshold. 120 opcos × 30-50% opt-in = 36-60 recurring customers. Proof model works with smart buyer.

3. **Three-layer map live** - Lyra (L1: customer relationships) + Remix (L2: strategy/design) + opcos (L3: FDEs). Customer archetype instantiation with structural blindness to L2.

4. **Channel/distribution archetype** - Remix IP sold through Lyra's MSP network to SMBs. Opcos execute; Remix stays advisory. Third exit archetype: "be sales/delivery engine for Remix IP."

5. **Capital map validation** - Evergreen/Alpine is second permanent-hold PE in SMB space. Confirms PE platforms are natural layer-2 advisory partners.
